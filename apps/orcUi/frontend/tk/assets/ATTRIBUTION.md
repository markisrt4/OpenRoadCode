  `1bb2f6e6ea50f920de8d2f3d273ae48564a5eaee`, rendered in brand blue.
  Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/1bb2f6e6ea50f920de8d2f3d273ae48564a5eaee/icons/waze.svg

  Brand trademark rights still apply.
- `order.svg` / `order.png`: Lucide shopping-bag icon, commit
  `3fef7aca71a50acc54bd2f24f0cc4747c22aa6d2`, rendered in white for the primary Order control.
  Source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/3fef7aca71a50acc54bd2f24f0cc4747c22aa6d2/icons/shopping-bag.svg
  See [Lucide license](LUCIDE-LICENSE).

PNG assets are normalized copies of the reviewed SVG sources. Existing
navigation/Earth/3D artwork remains unchanged.

The compact app-status indicator draws a four-color Google Play triangle and
download/check/unknown/web badges with Tk canvas primitives; no external image
service or new asset download is used. Google Play trademark rights apply.
